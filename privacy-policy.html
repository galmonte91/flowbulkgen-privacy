Privacy Policy

Flow Bulk Gen Chrome Extension — Last updated: June 25, 2025

Overview

Flow Bulk Gen is a Chrome extension that automates bulk image generation on Google Flow. It runs entirely on your device. We do not operate any servers, and we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

What the extension does

  • Reads prompts you type into the extension's side panel
  • Injects those prompts into the Google Flow editor on the active tab
  • Waits for images to be generated by Google Flow
  • Downloads the resulting images to your local computer

Data we do not collect

Flow Bulk Gen does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Your prompts or any text you enter
  • The images generated or downloaded
  • Browsing history or activity
  • Personal information of any kind

Local storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.local to remember your prompts and toggle settings (random delay, bulk download) between sessions. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Permissions explained

  • activeTab / tabs — to read the URL of your current tab and confirm you are on Google Flow
  • scripting — to inject prompts into the Google Flow editor and detect generated images
  • downloads — to save generated images to your computer
  • sidePanel — to display the extension's control panel
  • storage — to save your prompts and settings locally
  • host_permissions (labs.google/*) — required to run scripts on the Google Flow page

Third-party services

This extension interacts with Google Flow (labs.google), which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We have no control over how Google handles data on their platform.

Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
